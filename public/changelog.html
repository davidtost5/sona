Changelog

Everything shipped to Sona, newest first.

Nov 2, 2026

Billing moves to Polar

Paid plans are sold through Polar, which handles payment, VAT and invoices. Cancel or change your plan any time from Settings → Billing.

Improved
Oct 26, 2026

New logo and a better phone menu

Sona’s new mark is a single classical column. On phones, the menu now opens as a full sheet with a short description under each link.

New
Oct 23, 2026

Light and dark mode switch

Choose light or dark from the switch in the corner of the homepage, and Sona remembers it across the site. Buttons and tabs were redesigned, and Sona’s emails have a new look.

NewImproved
Oct 20, 2026

A homepage that shows the product

The homepage opens on a working tour: browse real outlier posts, decode one with the same engine as the app, and see how a draft and a week of posts come together.

Improved
Oct 13, 2026

Manage billing and clearer terms

Cancel any time from Settings → Billing or the account menu. The terms now spell out renewal, cancellation and refunds, and Sona is for people aged 16 and over.

New
Oct 13, 2026

Faster, smoother pages

Fonts now load from Sona’s own server, pages scroll smoothly, and the phone menus on Docs, Brand and Changelog work again.
