Answers to what people usually ask.

Most questions are answered here. If yours isn't, the form above reaches me directly.

Signing in

Sona doesn't use passwords. Enter your email on the sign-in page and type the 6-digit code we email you. If the code doesn't arrive within a minute, check your spam folder, then send a new one. The same two steps create an account if you don't have one yet.

Billing and cancelling

Paid plans are sold through Polar, which handles payment, VAT and invoices. You can change or cancel your plan any time from Settings, then Billing, inside the app. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the terms explain refunds and the EU and UK right of withdrawal.

Your data

You can export what you've saved from Settings, then Data. To delete your account and everything in it, write to me from the email you signed up with and I'll do it. The privacy policy lists who processes your data and why.

Reporting a bug

Tell me which page you were on, what you clicked, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot and your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) help a lot. If something stopped working after a change, the changelog shows what shipped and when.

Using Sona in Claude

Creator Pro connects Sona to Claude, so you can ask about the posts, hooks and drafts you've saved without leaving the chat. Sona in Claude walks through the setup step by step.

Ideas, press and partnerships

Feature ideas are welcome, and the best ones usually start with a post you wanted to study and couldn't. For press or partnerships, say who you are and what you have in mind, and I'll reply from david@buildwithsona.com.
