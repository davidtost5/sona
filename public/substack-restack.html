What restack means on Substack.

How restacking works, why it matters for growth, and how to use it well, whether you’re sharing others or hoping to be shared.

By David Tost, maker of Sona. Published 28 November 2026.

A restack is Substack’s way of sharing. When you restack a post or a note, it appears in the Notes feed of the people who follow you, with your name on it. You can restack as it is, or add a line of your own, which works like a quote.

What restack means on Substack

  • Restack a post: share someone’s article to your followers in Notes.
  • Restack a note: pass a short note along to your followers.
  • Restack with a comment: add your own words on top, so your followers see why you shared it.

It’s the closest thing Substack has to a retweet or a repost, and it’s one of the main ways writing travels beyond the people who already subscribe.

Why restacks matter for growth

Every restack puts a post or note in front of a new group of readers. When writers in your niche restack you, their followers see your name next to something they chose to recommend, which is a strong reason to look. That’s why many writers say Substack Notes and restacks bring them more new subscribers than anything else inside Substack.

How to restack well

  1. Add a reason. One line on what you took from it beats a bare restack. It tells your followers something about you too.
  2. Quote the best sentence. Highlighting one line gives people a reason to open the whole thing.
  3. Restack writers you actually read. Generous, honest sharing is noticed, and often returned.
  4. Don’t only restack yourself. A feed of self-promotion gets ignored.

How to get restacked more

  • Write one idea per note, with the point in the first line.
  • Include a line worth quoting in every post: a clear opinion, a number or a short rule.
  • Reply thoughtfully to other writers. People restack those they recognise.
  • Look at which of your notes and posts got far more restacks than usual, and do more of that. More in how to grow on Substack.

Questions people ask

What does restack mean on Substack?
Restacking shares a post or note with the people who follow you in Substack Notes, with your name on it. You can add your own comment, like a quote.
Is a restack the same as a repost?
Mostly, yes. It works like a retweet or repost on other networks, and you can add a comment of your own.
Do restacks help you grow on Substack?
Yes. Each restack shows your writing to a new group of readers, which is one of the main ways writers are discovered inside Substack.
Should I add a comment when I restack?
Usually. A short line on why it is worth reading makes the restack more useful to your followers and says something about you.
