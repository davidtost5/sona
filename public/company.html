Sona is made by one person

Sona is an independent tool built by David Tost. There is no team, office or investor behind it: just the product, and the person who answers your email.

Why Sona exists

Most advice about what to post is guesswork. Sona starts from evidence instead: public posts on X and LinkedIn that did far better than their creator usually does, and how each one is built. You learn the pattern, then write your own version in your own words. Sona never writes your posts for you.

How it is run

Sona is run by David Tost as an individual; it is not a registered company yet. Payments go through Polar as merchant of record, sign-in through Clerk, and your work is stored in Supabase. The privacy policy and terms have the details.

Get in touch

Email david@buildwithsona.com, use the contact form, or find David on X and LinkedIn. Every message gets a personal reply.
