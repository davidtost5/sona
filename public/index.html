Questions

Will my posts end up sounding like AI?

No. Sona doesn't write your posts. It puts what's working, the pattern behind it and your own playbook next to the editor, and you do the writing.

Where do the outlier posts come from?

They're public posts, picked by hand and checked against how the creator usually performs, so a post only counts as an outlier if it beat that creator's normal. The feed is small and grows every week.

Which platforms does it cover?

The feed starts with X and LinkedIn. Decoding works on a post from anywhere: paste the text and Sona reads it.

Is there a free trial?

The Free plan is free for as long as you like, with no card. Paid plans don't have a time-limited trial.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Open Settings, then Billing in the app, or Manage billing in your account menu. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until you cancel.

Are my drafts and playbook private?

Yes. They live in your workspace and aren't shared with other users. We don't sell your data and don't train models on your writing.
