Substack welcome email examples.

The one email every subscriber opens. What to include, three examples to adapt, and how to turn a new subscriber into a regular reader.

By David Tost, maker of Sona. Published 28 November 2026.

Your welcome email is the one email every subscriber opens. It arrives seconds after they said yes, while they still remember why. A good one sets expectations, points to your best work and starts a conversation. Most writers leave Substack’s default in place and miss that moment.

What a good Substack welcome email does

  1. Says thank you, briefly. One line is enough.
  2. Repeats the promise: what they’ll get and how often.
  3. Points to two or three of your best posts, so new readers see your strongest work first.
  4. Asks one easy question they can answer by replying. Replies build trust and help your emails reach the inbox.

Substack welcome email examples

These examples were written for this guide. Swap in your own promise, posts and question.

The short and personal

  • Thanks for subscribing. Every Thursday you’ll get one pricing page taken apart, and what you can copy from it. Start with these two: [post] and [post]. Quick question: what are you selling right now? Just hit reply. I read every answer.

The start-here guide

  • Welcome. If you’re new, here’s where to start: the most-read post, [post]; the one readers forward most, [post]; and the one I’m proudest of, [post]. New posts arrive every other Monday. If there’s a topic you’d like covered, reply and tell me.

The expectation setter

  • You’re in. Here’s the deal: one essay a week, never more, and nothing sold to you without a clear label. If it ever stops being useful, unsubscribing takes one click and I won’t take it personally. To start, read [post].

Tips that make welcome emails work

  • Keep it under 150 words. It’s a handshake, not an essay.
  • Use your own voice. Write it the way you would write to one person.
  • Link to posts, not your homepage. A specific post gets read.
  • Update it every few months as your best posts change.

Questions people ask

What should a Substack welcome email say?
Thank the reader briefly, repeat what they will get and how often, point to two or three of your best posts, and ask one easy question they can answer by replying.
How long should a Substack welcome email be?
Short: usually under 150 words. It works best as a quick, personal hello with a clear next step.
Why ask a question in a welcome email?
Replies start a relationship with the reader and are a good signal for email delivery, which helps your future posts reach the inbox.
Can I change my Substack welcome email later?
Yes. Update it whenever your best posts change, so new subscribers always see your strongest work first.
