Edit a Substack post after publishing.

You can change a published post at any time. Here’s how, what happens to the email that was already sent, and how to handle mistakes that matter.

By David Tost, maker of Sona. Published 18 November 2026.

Yes, you can edit a Substack post after publishing it. The web version updates when you save, so anyone who opens the post from now on sees the new text. The email that already went out does not change.

How to edit a published Substack post

  1. Open your publication’s dashboard and go to your posts.
  2. Find the post among your published posts and open it in the editor.
  3. Make your changes: text, images, title or subtitle.
  4. Use the button at the top of the editor to update the live post.
  5. Open the post’s web page to check the change is there.

What happens to the email that was already sent

Nothing. An email is a copy delivered to each inbox at the moment you published, and it can’t be changed afterwards. Readers who open the email see the original; readers who click through to the web page see your edit.

If the mistake matters, such as a wrong date, price or link, say so: add a short correction note at the top of the web version, and mention it at the start of your next post or in a note.

Edits to be careful with

  • The web address. Changing a post’s URL slug can break links that people have already shared. Leave it alone unless you have to.
  • The title. It’s fine to sharpen a title, but readers searching for the old one may be confused. Keep the meaning the same.
  • Big changes to the argument. If you change your mind, write a follow-up post rather than rewriting history. Readers trust writers who show their corrections.

Catch mistakes before you publish

  • Send yourself a test email and read it on your phone. Most typos show up on the small screen.
  • Click every link in the test email.
  • Read the first paragraph out loud. If it stumbles, so will readers.
  • Schedule the post for later instead of publishing straight away, so you can look at it once more with fresh eyes.

Questions people ask

Can you edit a Substack post after publishing?

Does editing a Substack post resend the email?
No. Updating a published post changes the web version only. The original email stays as it was in readers’ inboxes.
Can I change the URL of a Substack post?
You can change the slug, but links people have already shared may stop working, so avoid it unless you need to.
