Substack vs Medium, compared for writers.

How the two platforms differ on audience, earning and discovery, and how to decide which one suits the way you write.

By David Tost, maker of Sona. Published 28 November 2026.

Substack and Medium both let you publish writing online, but they make money and find readers in very different ways. The short version: on Substack you build your own list of readers; on Medium you write for the platform’s readers.

Substack vs Medium at a glance

CompareSubstackMedium
Main ideaA newsletter: posts go to subscribers by email and live on the webA reading platform: articles live on Medium and are shown to its readers
Your audienceYour subscribers, and you can export their emailsMedium’s readers and your followers there
How you earnYou set a price for paid subscriptions; Substack takes a shareMedium pays members of its partner program from its own subscriptions
DiscoveryNotes, recommendations from other writers and restacksMedium’s feeds, tags and publications
Best forBuilding a long-term relationship with a defined audienceReaching new readers without building a list first

When Substack is the better choice

  • You want readers you can reach directly, by email, for years.
  • You may charge for your writing at a price you choose.
  • You write regularly on one topic for a specific reader.

When Medium is the better choice

  • You write occasional, standalone articles on many topics.
  • You want readers to find you without promoting each piece yourself.
  • You don’t want to run a newsletter or keep a schedule.

Can you use both?

Yes. Some writers publish on Substack first and later adapt their best pieces for Medium, with a link back to subscribe. Check each platform’s current rules on republishing and canonical links before you do, and keep Substack as home if building a list is the goal.

Questions people ask

Is Substack better than Medium?
It depends on your goal. Substack is better for building your own email list and charging for subscriptions. Medium is better for reaching its existing readers without running a newsletter.
Can you make money on Substack and Medium?
Yes, in different ways. On Substack you set a price for paid subscriptions. On Medium, writers in its partner program are paid from Medium’s own memberships.
Do you own your audience on Substack?
Your subscribers’ emails can be exported from Substack, so you can keep your readers if you move. On Medium, your audience is your followers on the platform.
Can I post the same article on Substack and Medium?
Many writers do, usually publishing on Substack first. Check each platform’s current rules on republishing before you do.
