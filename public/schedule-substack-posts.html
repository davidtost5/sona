Schedule Substack posts, and plan your notes.

How to schedule a post, what to do with notes, when to send, and a routine for writing ahead so your newsletter shows up on time.

By David Tost, maker of Sona. Published 28 November 2026.

Yes, you can schedule Substack posts. Scheduling is the easiest way to keep a steady rhythm: write when you have time, and let the post go out on the day readers expect it.

How to schedule a Substack post

  1. Write your post and send yourself a test email.
  2. Go to publish it, and choose who it goes to: everyone, paid subscribers or the web only.
  3. Instead of publishing now, choose to schedule it, and pick the date and time.
  4. Confirm. The post now waits among your scheduled posts, where you can still edit it, move it or publish it early.

Can you schedule notes on Substack?

Substack’s scheduling is built around posts. Check the note composer in your version of Substack for a schedule option; if it isn’t there, plan notes ahead instead:

  • Keep a running list of note ideas and finished drafts in your notes app.
  • Post them when your readers are usually around, ideally in the same few minutes you reply to others.
  • If you try a third-party scheduler, check who makes it and what access to your account it asks for before you sign in with it.

Notes reward conversation, and a note that goes out while you’re away can’t get replies from you. That’s a good reason to post them yourself even when you write them ahead. More in our guide to Substack Notes.

When to schedule your posts

  • Same day, same time. Readers learn when to expect you, which matters more than any perfect hour.
  • When your readers read. Think about where they live and when they check email, and test a couple of options over a month.
  • Not on a day you’re unavailable. The first hour of replies is a good time to be around.

A simple batch-writing routine

  1. Keep a list of post ideas. Our Substack newsletter ideas are a good start.
  2. Once a month, outline the next four posts.
  3. Write one or two in a single session, and schedule them as soon as they’re done.
  4. Leave one slot open for something timely.

Questions people ask

Can you schedule Substack posts?
Yes. When you go to publish a post, choose to schedule it and pick a date and time. You can still edit, move or publish it early until it goes out.
Can you schedule notes on Substack?
Scheduling on Substack is built around posts. If your note composer has no schedule option, draft notes ahead and post them yourself, ideally when you can reply to comments.
What is the best time to send a Substack newsletter?
The time your own readers check email, kept the same every week. Consistency matters more than finding a perfect hour.
Can I edit a scheduled Substack post?
Yes. Scheduled posts can be edited, rescheduled or published early until they go out.
