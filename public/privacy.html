Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This is a summary of our current practice, published so nothing on this site links nowhere. It is not yet a complete legal document — the full privacy policy is being finalised. For anything not covered here, email david@buildwithsona.com and we will answer directly.

Who we are

Sona is run by David Tost, an individual, who is responsible for your data. It is not yet a registered company. Contact: david@buildwithsona.com.

What we collect

If you request access or contact us, we collect the name, email address and the details you type into that form. If you create an account, we store your email address and the content you create in Sona — your saved ideas, lists, drafts and playbook. If you buy a plan, Polar, our merchant of record, collects your payment and billing details. We receive and store your email address, your Polar customer reference, your plan, and whether your subscription is active or cancelled. We never receive your full card number.

Why we collect it

To reply to you, to give you access to your account, and to keep your work available to you across sessions. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to train models.

Who processes it

We use third-party providers to run the product. Today those are Clerk (accounts and sign-in), Supabase (your stored content), Vercel (hosting), Polar (payments, tax and invoices, as merchant of record), and Spacemail or Resend (email delivery). Each processes data on our behalf under its own terms.

Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Withdraw consent and close your account at any time.

Email david@buildwithsona.com and we will action any of the above.

Children

Sona is not for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has given us their data, email us and we will delete it.

Cookies

We use a session token to keep you signed in. We do not run third-party advertising or cross-site tracking.
